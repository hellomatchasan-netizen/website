Matcha San is a small matcha and coffee bar in Melbourne.

Every drink is whisked to order. The menu is short on purpose: five drinks we make well, with homemade strawberry jam, matcha cream foam and sea salt cream.

You'll find us at farmers markets and private events around Melbourne. Follow @matchasan.melb to see where we'll be next.
